Implemented Classification Architecture

Deterministic rule sequence executed on incoming sensor signals

  1. Priority 1 — Metal Check: If the inductive metal detection sensor registers a metallic/ferrous object, the item is immediately routed to the Metal compartment.
  2. Priority 2 — Moisture / Wetness Check: If metal is not detected, but the wetness/moisture sensor exceeds the threshold (≥ 45.0%), the item is routed to the Wet compartment.
  3. Priority 3 — Residual Dry Waste: When neither metal nor wetness is detected, the item is directed to the Dry compartment (e.g. paper, dry packaging, plastics).
Scope Notice: The prototype classifies strictly into Metal, Wet, or Dry based on physical inductive and moisture sensor triggers. It does not perform optical material identification or chemical classification.
